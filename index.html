    Common questions

    Capital Region chauffeur service, clearly explained.

    What areas does Empire Elite Rides serve?

    We serve communities throughout Albany, Rensselaer, and Saratoga counties, as well as Glens Falls and Lake George, New York. Long-distance and interstate transportation can also be requested through our booking form.

    What vehicle is used for chauffeur service?

    Choose during booking between our spacious black 2017 Ford Expedition Limited and our quiet Whisper Blue Metallic 2025 Lincoln Nautilus Hybrid.

    Do you provide Albany airport transportation?

    Yes. Scheduled airport pickups and drop-offs are available, including transportation to and from Albany International Airport.

    How do I request a reservation?

    Use our online booking form or call 860-326-1089. Every request is personally reviewed and confirmed.

    Is the vehicle cleaned before service?

    Yes. Every fleet vehicle is washed, detailed, and prepared before service for a polished and comfortable arrival.
